# QuizWhiz — Quiz Content Bank

Five quizzes × 10 questions. Difficulty ramps from **Easy → Medium → Hard** within each quiz. Every question has exactly **four choices** and **exactly one correct answer**, plus an explanation for the after-answer reveal.

*Reviewed for: single correct answer, unambiguous wording, no accidental duplicates among distractors.*

---

## 1. Math Quiz 🧮

*Tests: arithmetic, percentages, basic algebra, geometry fundamentals.*

| # | Difficulty | Question | Choices | Correct | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy | What is 7 × 8? | 54 / 56 / 58 / 64 | 56 | 7 × 8 = 56; a common slip is 54 (7×8 mixed up with 6×9). |
| 2 | Easy | What is 15% of 200? | 15 / 25 / 30 / 45 | 30 | 10% of 200 is 20; half of that (5%) is 10; 20 + 10 = 30. |
| 3 | Easy | What is the value of π rounded to two decimal places? | 3.12 / 3.14 / 3.16 / 3.18 | 3.14 | π ≈ 3.14159, which rounds to 3.14. |
| 4 | Easy | Solve: x + 7 = 12 | x = 3 / x = 5 / x = 7 / x = 19 | x = 5 | Subtract 7 from both sides: x = 12 − 7 = 5. |
| 5 | Easy | What is the square root of 144? | 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 | 12 | 12 × 12 = 144. |
| 6 | Medium | The angles inside any triangle always add up to how many degrees? | 90 / 180 / 270 / 360 | 180 | One of Euclid's basics: every triangle's interior angles sum to exactly 180°. |
| 7 | Medium | What is 2⁵ (2 to the power of 5)? | 10 / 16 / 25 / 32 | 32 | 2×2×2×2×2 = 32. (25 is the trap for reading it as 2×5.) |
| 8 | Medium | What is the next prime number after 7? | 8 / 9 / 11 / 13 | 11 | 8 and 9 are divisible (2, 3); 11 has no divisors other than 1 and itself. |
| 9 | Hard | In a right triangle, the two shorter sides are 6 and 8. How long is the hypotenuse? | 9 / 10 / 12 / 14 | 10 | Pythagoras: 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100, and √100 = 10. |
| 10 | Hard | A shirt costs $40 and is on sale at 25% off. What is the sale price? | $10 / $15 / $30 / $35 | $30 | 25% of 40 = 10, so the price drops by $10: 40 − 10 = $30. |

## 2. Science Quiz 🔬

*Tests: astronomy, chemistry basics, human biology, physics fundamentals.*

| # | Difficulty | Question | Choices | Correct | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy | Which planet is known as the Red Planet? | Venus / Mars / Jupiter / Mercury | Mars | Iron oxide (rust) in Martian soil gives it a reddish color. |
| 2 | Easy | What is the chemical formula H₂O better known as? | Oxygen / Salt / Water / Hydrogen peroxide | Water | Two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom — water. |
| 3 | Easy | What gas do plants absorb from the air for photosynthesis? | Oxygen / Nitrogen / Carbon dioxide / Hydrogen | Carbon dioxide | Plants take in CO₂ and, using sunlight, turn it into glucose and oxygen. |
| 4 | Medium | How many bones are in the adult human body? | 106 / 206 / 306 / 406 | 206 | Babies start with about 270; bones fuse as we grow, ending at 206. |
| 5 | Medium | What is the powerhouse of the cell? | Nucleus / Ribosome / Mitochondria / Cell membrane | Mitochondria | Mitochondria convert food energy into ATP, the cell's fuel. |
| 6 | Medium | What is the largest organ of the human body? | Liver / Brain / Skin / Heart | Skin | Skin covers your whole body and is the largest organ by area and weight. |
| 7 | Medium | What is the chemical symbol for gold? | Au / Ag / Gd / Go | Au | From the Latin name *aurum*. Ag is silver. |
| 8 | Hard | Roughly how fast does light travel in a vacuum? | 3,000 km/s / 30,000 km/s / 300,000 km/s / 3,000,000 km/s | 300,000 km/s | Light speed is about 299,792 km/s — roughly 300,000 km/s, or about 7.5 laps of Earth per second. |
| 9 | Hard | Which of these is NOT a state of matter? | Solid / Liquid / Gas / Energy | Energy | Solid, liquid, and gas are classic states of matter; energy is a property, not a state. |
| 10 | Hard | What does DNA stand for? | Deoxyribonucleic acid / Dinucleic acid / Double nucleic acid / Diribonucleic acid | Deoxyribonucleic acid | DNA carries the genetic instructions for living organisms. |

## 4. Geography Quiz 🌍

*Tests: capitals, oceans, continents, rivers, mountains, and hemispheres.*

| # | Difficulty | Question | Choices | Correct | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy | What is the capital city of France? | Lyon / Marseille / Paris / Nice | Paris | Paris has been France's capital for centuries and is its largest city. |
| 2 | Easy | Which is the largest ocean on Earth? | Atlantic / Indian / Arctic / Pacific | Pacific | The Pacific covers about a third of the planet's surface — larger than all land combined. |
| 3 | Easy | The Sahara Desert is located on which continent? | Asia / Africa / Australia / South America | Africa | The Sahara spans North Africa and is the world's largest hot desert. |

| 8 | Medium | Mount Everest sits on the border of Nepal and which country? | India / China / Bhutan / Pakistan | China | Everest straddles the Nepal–China border in the Himalayas. |

## 5. English Quiz 📚

*Tests: vocabulary, grammar, parts of speech, literary devices.*

| # | Difficulty | Question | Choices | Correct | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy | Which word is a synonym for "happy"? | Angry / Tired / Joyful / Gloomy | Joyful | Synonyms share a meaning: joyful and happy both mean feeling glad. |
| 2 | Easy | What is the plural of "child"? | Childs / Childes / Children / Childrens | Children | "Child" has an irregular plural, like "man → men." |
| 3 | Easy | What is the past tense of the verb "go"? | Goed / Gone / Went / Going | Went | "Go" is irregular: go / went / gone. "Gone" needs a helper verb (has gone). |
| 4 | Medium | Which sentence is grammatically correct? | She don't like apples. / She doesn't like apples. / She didn't liked apples. / She not like apples. | She doesn't like apples. | Third-person singular uses "doesn't" with the base verb. |
| 5 | Medium | In the sentence "The quick brown fox jumps," which word is the noun? | quick / brown / fox / jumps | fox | A noun names a person, place, or thing — the fox is the thing here. |
| 6 | Medium | Which word in "She sang beautifully" is the adverb? | She / sang / beautifully / (none — there is no adverb) | beautifully | Adverbs often end in -ly and describe how the verb is done. |
| 7 | Medium | Which of these is an example of alliteration? | The rain fell softly. / Peter Piper picked a peck. / Run quickly! / A bright blue sky. | Peter Piper picked a peck. | Alliteration repeats the same beginning sound across neighboring words. |
| 8 | Hard | What is a word with the opposite meaning of another word called? | Synonym / Antonym / Homonym / Acronym | Antonym | Antonyms are opposites, like "hot" and "cold." |
| 9 | Hard | A simile compares two things using which words? | and / but / like or as / is or was | like or as | "Brave as a lion" is a simile; without "like/as" it becomes a metaphor. |
| 10 | Hard | What is a group of lines in a poem called? | Stanza / Chapter / Paragraph / Scene | Stanza | Stanzas are the "paragraphs" of poetry, separated by blank lines. |
